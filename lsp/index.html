Welcome to LSP Planner

Design a logistics network like a game. Three steps and you have a score.

  1. 1 · Place sites. The level fixes the factory and the customer zones (seeded demand). You add the middle: central DCs, regional DCs, cross-docks.
  2. 2 · Connect lanes. Pick Draw lane, click site A, then site B. Set each lane to Full truckload (pay per truck) or Parcel/LTL (pay per tonne-km) — and each DC to push or pull replenishment.
  3. 3 · Evaluate. One click computes weekly cost, service vs the lead-time target and a CO2 estimate — scored 0–100 with visible weights, plus pass/fail against the level's targets.

The map is an abstract region — no real country, company or network. All demand is synthetic and seeded; costs and CO2 are estimates with stated assumptions. Fully offline.
