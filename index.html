Welcome to WarehouseTwin

A tiny, honest warehouse digital twin. Three steps and you are running.

  1. 1 · Place racks & docks. Click a palette item, then click the floor. Drag to move, keep a valid aisle between rack rows.
  2. 2 · Pick a strategy. Choose Random or ABC 80/20 slotting on the right.
  3. 3 · Run the sim. Hit Run to see throughput, pick travel and storage fill. Same seed → same result.

All data is synthetic and seeded. Icons and code are original/open. Nothing is sent anywhere — it runs fully offline.
