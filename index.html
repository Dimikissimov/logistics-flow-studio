Push tops pick faces up on a periodic forecast (can overstock and run dry between cycles); pull replenishes on consumption via a reorder point. Both are simulated on the pick-face inventory — see the stockout / overstock KPIs.

Welcome to WarehouseTwin

A tiny, honest warehouse digital twin. Three steps and you are running.

  1. 1 · Place racks & docks. Click a palette item, then click the floor. Drag to move, keep a valid aisle between rack rows. Chain conveyors from storage to a pack station and the outbound dock — flow arrows show what is connected. The starter layout begins with its conveyor and stations disconnected on purpose: fixing its five flow warnings is your first exercise.
  2. 2 · Pick strategies. Choose slotting/picking (Random, ABC, Zone, Batch, Wave) and push vs pull replenishment on the right — or load the MRO preset for a full layout.
  3. 3 · Run the sim. Hit Run to see throughput, pick travel, storage fill, stockouts and overstock. Same seed → same result. Toggle Heatmap above the floor to see where the pickers walk, and compare experiments in Run history.

All data starts synthetic and seeded — and in the full version you can make it yours: Import your data (left panel) runs the sim on your own article/order CSVs, and Floor plan underlay lets you trace racks over a photo of your real hall. Icons and code are original/open. Nothing is sent anywhere — it runs fully offline.
